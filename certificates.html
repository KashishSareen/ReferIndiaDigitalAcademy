Eligibility

How Certificates Are Awarded

Learners who successfully complete their enrolled course and fulfill the applicable course requirements will receive a Course Completion Certificate from Refer India Digital Academy.

Certificate eligibility may depend on:

  • Course completion
  • Required participation/attendance
  • Completion of assignments or practical projects
  • Applicable assessment requirements

Important Disclaimer

The certificate recognizes successful completion of the training program.

It does not by itself represent a government license, academic degree, professional qualification or guarantee of employment.
