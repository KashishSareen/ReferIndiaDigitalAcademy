Certification

Course Completion Certificate

Learners who successfully complete their enrolled course and fulfill the applicable course requirements will receive a Course Completion Certificate from Refer India Digital Academy.

Certificate eligibility may depend on:

  • Course completion
  • Required participation/attendance
  • Completion of assignments or practical projects
  • Applicable assessment requirements

Important Disclaimer

The certificate recognizes successful completion of the training program.

It does not by itself represent a government license, academic degree, professional qualification or guarantee of employment.

Internship Opportunity

Gain Practical Experience

After successfully completing the course, eligible learners may receive an internship opportunity, subject to the applicable requirements and availability.

The internship is intended to provide practical exposure and work-related experience that complements the learner's training.

Internship details

Internship Eligibility

Learners may need to:

  • Successfully complete the course.
  • Complete required assignments/projects.
  • Maintain satisfactory participation.
  • Complete applicable assessments.
  • Follow internship rules and guidelines.

Important Disclaimer

Course completion does not automatically guarantee an internship or employment.

Where an internship is offered, the learner will receive information about the internship duration, role, responsibilities, working arrangements and applicable terms before the internship begins.
